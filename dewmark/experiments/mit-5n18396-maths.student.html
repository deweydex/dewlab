Notation

Use whatever feels natural — the only thing that matters is being consistent within each answer.

Powers
x^2, x**2, and “x to the power 2” all mean the same thing. x**2 is the same thing.
Fractions
Write a/b. Use brackets for compound tops or bottoms: (x+3)/(x-1).
Square roots
Write sqrt(x) or surd form like 4√2..
Derivatives
f'(x), dy/dx, or d/dx are all fine — say once at the top of each calculus answer which you’re using.
Trig and degrees
The calculator uses sind/cosd/tand for degree input. To convert manually: multiply degrees by π/180.
Intervals
x < 2 or (-inf, 2) are both fine — pick one per answer and stick to it.
Infinity
Write +inf or -inf when describing what a function approaches.
Working
A correct final answer with no working shown may not receive full marks.

Counting and Probability
Factorial n! = n × (n−1) × ··· × 2 × 1
Arrangements — order matters P(n,r) = n! / (n−r)!
Selections — order doesn’t matter C(n,r) = n! / (r! (n−r)!)
0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
If A and B cannot both happen P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)
If A and B are independent P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
Expected occurrences E = (number of tries) × P(event)
