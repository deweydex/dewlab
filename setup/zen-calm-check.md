> **A calm check.** Stop for a moment. How do you feel? Is this still
> interesting? Or do you feel frustrated: annoyed, tired or stuck?
>
> If you feel frustrated, the last step was too big. The problem is the
> step, not you. You can do one of three things:
>
> - Return to the last step that was clear. Read it again slowly.
> - Open "stuck? here are some steps" under the next question.
> - Stop for today. The page keeps your place.
