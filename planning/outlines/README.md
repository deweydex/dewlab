# Outlines

One file per tutorial that does not exist yet. Each says what the tutorial is
for, what its sections would be, and what the runnable cells would do — enough
that somebody could sit down and write it, and enough that Josh can disagree
with the shape before any of it is written.

They are outlines, not drafts. Nothing here is student-facing yet, so the
register is ours rather than theirs.

## Two rules about size and naming

**A title should be plain.** Josh, 2026-08-23: *"in general, we should have
simpler ones rather than more complex ones."* "Lines and Distances" rather than
"Coordinate Geometry"; "How We Got Here" rather than "The Computing Time
Machine". The title names what a reader gets, in words they already have.

**A tutorial is as small as it can be without stopping being a tutorial.** Also
Josh, the same day, correcting an over-enthusiastic re-cut that had reached one
outcome per file: *"I'd rather they not be too short — they still need to
motivate, introduce and explain content, and give students a chance to work
through things."*

So the floor is the teaching rather than the outcome count. A tutorial has to
give a reason to care, introduce the idea, explain it, and leave room to
practise. Where two outcomes are one idea they stay together; where one outcome
is two activities it can split. `short` below means fewer sections, not less of
any of those four things.

`planning/curriculum/proposed.yaml` says where each one would go in the series
and why that position. `planning/CURRICULUM_MAP.md` shows what each would close.
